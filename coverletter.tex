During the job fair at my Faculty I was very excited about getting a chance to see your product in action, and also to speak with \\Mr Christof about your plans to establish a new branch in Warsaw.

Immediately, I knew that I've found a company, where my passion for machine learning and long hours put into studying it could be useful.
\lettersection{Why Me?}

As a soon to be graduate student, I specially chose machine learning and deep learning as a thing, that would be the core of my future career. For a long time, I have been fascinated by all the exciting possibilities that these fields of computer science could bring into our lives.

In order to be one of the people making these changes possible, I dug deeper into these topics with:
\begin{itemize}
\item{Taking many courses at my faculty that deal with ML and DL, such as: \textbf{Machine Learning Basics}, \textbf{Knowledge Discovery Methods}, \textbf{Biometric Authentication and Identification} but also on-line courses such as \textbf{Andrew Ng's Machine Learning course} or \textbf{Stanford's CS231n:  Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition}.}
\item{Extensive extra-curricular reading of articles and books about AI, image recognition, data regression and others.}
\item{Creating many smaller or bigger projects, such as simple implementation of neural network in Python or CIFAR-10 image classifier, in order to use all this theory in practice.}
\end{itemize}

\lettersection{Why Scandit?}
Scandit products show me, that your company also believes in using new technologies 
to improve existing solutions and doing it in a way, that many people would not have thought about.

Also, after talking with one of your co-founders, I have gotten a feeling, that working for you would not be a mere clock-punching, but rather a great opportunity to grow as a machine learning engineer.

I would love to put my passion and skills to work for you during the internship and possibly even after that. \\Can we schedule a call or a meeting to discuss it?

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.
